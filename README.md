# Piotr Trusiewicz

I’m a master’s student in Smart Aerospace and Autonomous Systems at Poznan University of Technology and Université Évry Paris-Saclay, following a double-degree programme.

I work on mechanical design, 3D-printed components and robot simulations. My projects include a remote-controlled boat, a mechanical iris for light control, a scissor jack CAD model and automation tasks in ABB RobotStudio.

I use this profile to document the designs, files and results of my work. Some projects were completed individually and others with a team. Each repository explains the project scope and my contribution.

I’m looking for an engineering internship from March 2027 as part of my master’s programme. I would welcome the opportunity to continue working with the company afterwards.
